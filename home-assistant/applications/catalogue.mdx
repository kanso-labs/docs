## Licences

The packaging is MIT licensed. The applications it packages keep their own terms, mostly a GNU licence or MIT, with some worth knowing before you install:

- **n8n** is fair-code, under its Sustainable Use License.
- **Plex Media Server** is proprietary and used under Plex's terms.
- **Tdarr** is free for personal use under a licence that forbids redistributing it, and **Seadexerr** carries no licence at all, which is why neither has an image. Your Home Assistant builds each one itself.

Each application's documentation records its licence and credits the packaging it was ported from, most often [hassio-addons](https://github.com/hassio-addons).
